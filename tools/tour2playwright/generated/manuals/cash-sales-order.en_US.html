Cash Sales Order

How to create a cash sales order in Odoo and take it all the way through delivery, invoice and payment.

Module: Sales · Language: English · Steps: 35

Part 1 — Create the quotation

  1. Open the Sales app. Click the Sales module in the main menu.
  2. Open Orders. Click Orders.
  3. Open Quotations. Click Quotations.
  4. Start a new quotation. Click the New button.
  5. Find the customer. Click the Customer field and start typing the customer's name.
  6. Select the customer. Click the matching customer in the dropdown list.
  7. Choose the payment method. In Order Payment Type, select Cash.
  8. Choose the quotation type. In Quotation Type, select Sales.

Part 2 — Add the product

  1. Add a line. In the order lines table, click Add a product.
  2. Search the product. Type the product name or code in the Product field (for example 02BB 023).
  3. Select the product. Click the product in the dropdown, for example [02BB 023] BALL BEARING 6202-2RS.
  4. Set the quantity. Click the Quantity cell and enter the quantity (default 1.0000).

Part 3 — Other Info tab

  1. Open Other Info. Click the Other Info tab.
  2. Open Payment Terms. Click the Payment Terms field. The default may be 90 Days.
  3. Pick Immediate Payment. Select Immediate Payment, because this is a cash order.
  4. Open Sales Team. Click the Sales Team field.
  5. Search more. Click Search More... at the bottom of the dropdown.
  6. Select the team. In the list, click Colombo Sales Centre.
  7. Open Warehouse. Click the Warehouse field.
  8. Search more. Click Search More....
  9. Select the warehouse. Click JAM Warehouse Ekala- (JM-EK).

Part 4 — Confirm and deliver

  1. Confirm the order. Click Confirm. The quotation becomes a sales order.
  2. Open the delivery. Click the Delivery smart button.
  3. Validate the delivery. Click Validate.
  4. Go back to the order. Click the order reference in the breadcrumb (for example S01881).

Part 5 — Invoice

  1. Create the invoice. Click Create Invoice.
  2. Confirm the invoice. Click Confirm on the invoice.
  3. Check the source order. Click the Sale Orders smart button to confirm the link back to the order.
  4. Open the invoice again. Click the Invoices smart button.

Part 6 — Register the payment

  1. Register payment. Click Register Payment.
  2. Open Journal. Click the Journal field (bank).
  3. Search more. Click Search More....
  4. Select the cash journal. Click Customer Receipts - Cash.
  5. Create the payment. Click Create Payment.
  6. Verify the links. Click the Sale Orders smart button, then the Invoices smart button, to check that order, invoice and payment are linked.
Notes
  • Steps 14–15: always use Immediate Payment for cash orders, not 90 Days.
  • Steps 24, 27 and 34 are real accounting actions and cannot be undone casually. Practise on a test database.
