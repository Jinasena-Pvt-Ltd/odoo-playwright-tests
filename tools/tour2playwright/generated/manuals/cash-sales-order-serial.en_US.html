Cash Sales Order - Serial

Step-by-step guide to creating a Cash sales order for a serial-number product: create the quotation, confirm it, add a new serial number on the delivery, validate the delivery, create the invoice and register the cash payment.

1Open the Sales app

On the Odoo home screen, click the Sales icon to open the Sales app.

Why: every sale starts in the Sales app. Check: the Sales menu (Orders, To Invoice, Products, Reporting...) appears across the top.

2Open the Orders menu

Click Orders in the top menu. A small dropdown opens.

Why: the Orders menu groups quotations and sales orders. Check: the dropdown shows Quotations.

3Open the Quotations list

Click Quotations in the dropdown.

Why: a quotation is the draft of a sale; it becomes a Sales Order when you confirm it. Check: the list of existing quotations opens.

4Start a new quotation

Click the New button at the top left.

Check: a blank quotation form opens.

5Find the customer

Click the Customer field and start typing the customer name (for example "abc").

Why: the list narrows as you type. Tip: for a Cash sale, use a Cash customer.

6Select the customer

Click the customer you want in the dropdown list.

Check: the customer name and address appear on the form, and the Invoice and Delivery Address fill in automatically. Watch out: a Cash customer cannot be changed to Credit later.

7Set the payment type to Cash

In Order Payment Type, choose Cash.

Why: this tells Odoo the customer pays immediately. Check: the field shows "Cash".

8Set the quotation type to Sales

In Quotation Type, choose Sales.

Check: the field shows "Sales". Watch out: the quotation cannot be saved if this field is empty.

9Add a product line

Under the Order Lines tab, click Add a product.

Check: a new empty line appears and the Product field is ready for typing.

10Open the Product field

Click the Product field on the new line. A dropdown with a few products opens.

Tip: the dropdown shows only the first few products; use "Search More..." to see them all.

11Click "Search More..."

At the bottom of the dropdown, click Search More....

Check: a larger window opens with a full product list and a search bar.

12Search for the product

In the search bar of that window, type the product code, for example EY20KP, and press Enter.

Tip: typing a part of the name or code is enough. Check: the list shrinks to the matching product.

13Select the serial-number product

Click the product [27PM LJ20] EY20KP (PETROL/KEROSENE) PUMP SHAFT ENGINE (LJ20).

Why: this product is tracked by serial number, so each unit needs its own serial number at delivery. Check: the product appears on the order line.

14Open the Other Info tab

Click the Other Info tab.

Check: payment terms, sales team and warehouse fields are visible.

15Open the Payment Terms field

Click the Payment Terms field.

Check: a dropdown of payment terms opens.

16Choose Immediate Payment

Click Immediate Payment.

Why: this is a Cash order, so payment is due straight away. Check: the field shows "Immediate Payment".

17Open the Sales Team field

Click the Sales Team field.

Check: a dropdown of sales teams opens.

18Click "Search More..."

Click Search More... at the bottom of the dropdown.

Check: a window with the full list of sales teams opens.

19Select the Sales Team

Click Colombo Sales Centre.

Check: the Sales Team field shows "Colombo Sales Centre" and the Warehouse is filled in automatically.

20Open the Warehouse field

Click the Warehouse field.

Tip: Odoo usually fills the warehouse from the Sales Team. If it already shows "JAM Warehouse Ekala- (JM-EK)", you can skip to the Confirm step.

21Click "Search More..."

If the dropdown shows it, click Search More....

Check: a window with the list of warehouses opens.

22Select the warehouse

Click JAM Warehouse Ekala- (JM-EK).

Check: the Warehouse field shows "JAM Warehouse Ekala- (JM-EK)".

23Confirm the order

Click the Confirm button at the top.

Why: this turns the quotation into a confirmed Sales Order and creates the delivery. Check: the status changes to "Sales Order" and a Delivery smart button appears.

24Open the delivery

Click the Delivery smart button at the top of the order.

Check: the delivery transfer (for example JM-EK/OUT/00001) opens.

25Open the serial number details

On the product line, click the list icon at the right end of the line.

Why: a serial-number product must have a serial number assigned before it can be delivered. Check: the "Open: Stock move" window opens.

26Add a serial number line

Click Add a line in that window.

Check: a list of serial numbers already in stock opens, with a **New** button.

27Create a new serial number

Click New.

Check: the "Create Move Line" window opens with a Lot/Serial Number field.

28Enter the new serial number

Click the Lot/Serial Number field and type the new serial number.

Tip: each serial number must be unique for the product.

29Create the serial number

Click Create "<your serial number>" in the dropdown.

Check: the serial number is accepted and the field shows it.

30Confirm with Enter

Press Enter in the Lot/Serial Number field.

Check: the serial number stays in the field.

31Save the move line

Click Save & Close.

Check: the Create Move Line window closes.

32Save the serial number

In the "Open: Stock move" window, click Save.

Check: the window closes and the line now shows the quantity.

33Save the delivery

Click the Save (cloud) icon at the top of the delivery.

Check: the delivery is saved with the serial number.

34Validate the delivery

Click Validate.

Why: this hands the goods to the customer and removes them from stock. Check: the delivery status becomes "Done". Watch out: Odoo blocks the delivery if the serial number has no stock.

35Go back to the sales order

Click the sales order number in the breadcrumb at the top left (for example S01881).

Check: you are back on the sales order.

36Create the invoice

Click Create Invoice.

Check: the invoice window or the draft invoice opens.

37Confirm the invoice

Click Confirm.

Why: this posts the invoice. Check: the invoice status becomes "Posted".

38Register the payment

Click Register Payment.

Check: the payment window opens.

39Create button (recorded step)

Click the create option highlighted at this point of the recording.

Note: this step was recorded as part of the serial-number dropdown, not the payment window. Review it with your administrator before using the manual as training.

40Open the Journal field

Click the Journal field.

Check: the list of journals opens.

41Choose the journal (cash)

Click the Journal field again and start typing the journal name.

Note: this step is labelled "Click the Serial number Product" in the recording, but it acts on the Journal field. Review before training use.

⚠ Adapted from the recorded tour — see REVIEW.md for details.

42Click "Search More..."

Click Search More... at the bottom of the dropdown.

Check: a window with the journals opens.

43Select Customer Receipts - Cash

Click Customer Receipts - Cash.

Why: a cash payment is recorded on the cash journal. Check: the Journal field shows "Customer Receipts - Cash".

44Create the payment

Click Create Payment.

Check: the payment is created and the invoice shows it as paid or in payment.

45Open the source sales order

Click the Sales Orders smart button.

Check: the original sales order opens.

46Open the invoice

Click the Invoices smart button.

Check: the invoice linked to the order opens.

47Save

Click Save in the window footer.

Check: the window closes.

48Open the serial-number product line

Click the product code 27PM LJ20 in the list.

Check: the product line opens.

49Save and close

Click Save & Close.

Check: the window closes and you are back on the record.
