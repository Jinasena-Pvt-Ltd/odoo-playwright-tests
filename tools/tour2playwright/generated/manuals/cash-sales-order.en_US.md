# Cash Sales Order — User Manual (English)

Step-by-step guide to selling to a cash customer: create the quotation, confirm it, deliver the goods, invoice the customer and record the cash payment.

Generated from an Odoo Tour Recorder export, replayed with Playwright, and screenshotted at every step.

---

## 1. Open the Sales app

From the Odoo home screen, click the **Sales** app.

![Step 1](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-010.png)

---

## 2. Go to Orders

In the top menu, click **Orders** to see the order options.

![Step 2](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-020.png)

---

## 3. Open Quotations

Click **Quotations** to see the list of existing quotations.

![Step 3](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-030.png)

---

## 4. Create a new quotation

Click the **New** button at the top left to start a blank quotation.

![Step 4](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-040.png)

---

## 5. Search for the customer

Click the **Customer** field and type the customer name (for example "abc"). Matching customers appear in a list.

![Step 5](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-050.png)

---

## 6. Choose the customer

Click the correct customer in the list to select them.

![Step 6](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-060.png)

---

## 7. Set the payment type to Cash

In **Order Payment Type**, choose **Cash**, because the customer pays on the spot.

![Step 7](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-070.png)

---

## 8. Set the quotation type

In **Quotation Type**, choose **Sales**.

![Step 8](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-080.png)

---

## 9. Add a product line

Under the order lines, click **Add a product** to add a new row.

![Step 9](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-090.png)

---

## 10. Search for the product

In the new row, type the product name or code (for example "02BB 023").

![Step 10](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-100.png)

---

## 11. Choose the product

Click the product in the list, for example **[02BB 023] BALL BEARING 6202-2RS**.

![Step 11](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-110.png)

---

## 12. Check the quantity

Click the **Quantity** cell. It starts at 1. Type a different number if the customer is buying more.

![Step 12](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-120.png)

---

## 13. Open the Other Info tab

Click the **Other Info** tab to fill in payment, team and warehouse details.

![Step 13](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-130.png)

---

## 14. Open Payment Terms

Click the **Payment Terms** field. A list of payment terms appears.

![Step 14](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-140.png)

---

## 15. Choose Immediate Payment

Select **Immediate Payment**. Cash orders are paid straight away, so do not use terms such as "90 Days".

![Step 15](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-150.png)

---

## 16. Open Sales Team

Click the **Sales Team** field. If your team is not in the short list, use "Search More..." in the next step.

![Step 16](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-160.png)

---

## 17. Search for more teams

Click **Search More...** at the bottom of the list to see all sales teams.

![Step 17](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-170.png)

---

## 18. Choose your sales team

Click your team, for example **Colombo Sales Centre**.

![Step 18](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-180.png)

---

## 19. Open Warehouse

Click the **Warehouse** field to choose where the goods will be shipped from.

![Step 19](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-181.png)

---

## 20. Search for more warehouses

Click **Search More...** to see all warehouses.

![Step 20](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-182.png)

---

## 21. Choose the warehouse

Click the warehouse, for example **JAM Warehouse Ekala- (JM-EK)**.

![Step 21](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-183.png)

---

## 22. Confirm the order

Click **Confirm**. The quotation becomes a confirmed Sales Order.

![Step 22](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-184.png)

---

## 23. Open the delivery

Click the **Delivery** button at the top of the order to open the delivery document.

![Step 23](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-185.png)

---

## 24. Validate the delivery

Click **Validate** to confirm the goods have been handed over to the customer.

![Step 24](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-186.png)

---

## 25. Go back to the order

Click the order number (for example **S01881**) at the top to return to the sales order.

![Step 25](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-187.png)

---

## 26. Start the invoice

Click **Create Invoice** to begin billing the customer.

![Step 26](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-188.png)

---

## 27. Create the invoice

In the window that opens, click **Create Invoice** again to confirm.

![Step 27](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-189.png)

---

## 28. Register the payment

On the invoice, click **Register Payment** to record the cash received.

![Step 28](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-192.png)

---

## 29. Choose the journal

Click the **Journal** field to choose where the payment is recorded.

![Step 29](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-193.png)

---

## 30. Search for more journals

Click **Search More...** to see all journals.

![Step 30](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-194.png)

---

## 31. Choose the cash journal

Select **Customer Receipts - Cash**.

![Step 31](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-195.png)

---

## 32. Create the payment

Click **Create Payment** to finish. The invoice is now paid.

![Step 32](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-196.png)

---

## 33. Return to the order

Click the **Sale Orders** button to go back to the sales order.

![Step 33](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-197.png)

---

## 34. Open the invoice again

Click the **Invoices** button to view the invoice one last time.

![Step 34](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-198.png)

---

## 35. Done — the invoice is paid

The invoice shows the green **In Payment** ribbon. The cash sale is complete.

![Step 35](../screenshots/cash-sales-order/step-199.png)

---
