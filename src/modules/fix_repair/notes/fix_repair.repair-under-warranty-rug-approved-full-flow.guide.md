# Repair User Guide — Factory
## Type 1: Under Warranty - RUG (Approved) — Full Flow

> Source: Odoo Tour Recorder export

## 1. Overview

This manual explains how to register and complete a **factory** repair for a product that is **under warranty** and approved under the **RUG** facility, from the Helpdesk ticket through factory repair, RUG approval, delivery and invoicing to dispatch.

### Ticket stages covered

`New` → `Sent to Factory` → `Received at Factory` → `Diagnosis` → `Estimation Sent to Customer` → `Estimation Approval Received` → `Repair Started` → `Repair Completed` → `Sent to Sales Centre` → `Received at Sales Centre` → `Handed Over to Customer`

### Before you start

- You have access to the Helpdesk, Field Service, Sales, Accounting and Inventory apps (the RUG approval is done by a user with R&D rights).
- The active company (top bar) is **Jinasena Agricultural Machinery (Pvt) Ltd.**
- The customer, the finished product (with its serial number, already sold through a sales order) and the spare parts already exist in Odoo.
- The customer has brought the item and a photo of the warranty card is available.

| Section | Steps | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1–12 | Open Helpdesk, create the ticket, classify it as Under Warranty - RUG, enter the serial number and attach the warranty card. |
| B | 13–15 | Return the item into stock and validate the receipt. |

## QA notes

- This **Under Warranty - RUG (Approved)** factory flow follows the earlier factory guides in the ticket, receipt, factory and diagnosis legs, but differs in these ways: the ticket needs a **Serial Number** and a **Warranty Card** image (instead of *Create Serial No*); the quotation goes through **Request RUG Approval → Approve RUG** and is then confirmed directly (no email, online signature or 50% deposit); there is **no payment or balance** to collect; and the final invoice is switched with **Change to RUG Account** before it is confirmed.
- The Serial Number field only offers serials already sold to a customer (a lot filter of `is_issued = true`). The tour typed `0630_001`; the automated run picks a sold serial with a clean history (this run used `0630_005`), because `0630_001` already holds many units piled up in *Virtual Locations/Repair/Ekala* from earlier unfinished runs, which makes the final Dispatch fail with "The serial number has already been assigned". The Product field is filled from the serial, so the tour has no separate Product step.
- The **Return** button (`fix_repair_action_direct_return`) raises "You may only return Done pickings" server-side but still creates the Returned Picking first. The automated run clicks the real button, falls back to the same call via `/web/dataset/call_kw` if no picking appears, and validates the receipt through RPC (the receipt's Validate button is unreliable under the Studio views).
