## 1. Overview

This manual explains how to register and complete a **branch** (Centre Repair) repair for a product that is **under warranty** but whose **RUG** request is **rejected**, from the Helpdesk ticket through the repair task, the RUG rejection, the customer-paid quotation, deposit and balance payments and delivery to dispatch.

### Ticket stages covered

`New` → `Diagnosis` → `Estimation Sent to Customer` → `Estimation Approval Received` → `Repair Started` → `Repair Completed` → `Handed Over to Customer`

### Before you start

- You have access to the Helpdesk, Field Service, Sales, Accounting and Inventory apps (the RUG decision is made by a user with R&D rights).
- The active company (top bar) is **Jinasena Agricultural Machinery (Pvt) Ltd.**
- The customer, the finished product (with its serial number, already sold through a sales order) and the spare parts already exist in Odoo.
- The customer has brought the item to the branch and a photo of the warranty card is available. The customer is prepared to pay for the repair if the RUG request is rejected.

## QA notes

- This **Branch** variant of the Under Warranty - RUG (Reject) flow uses **Job Location = Centre Repair**. After the item is received the ticket goes straight to **Plan Intervention** and the order generates a single outbound delivery transfer. After **Request RUG Approval**, **Reject RUG** makes the order customer-paid: the quotation is e-mailed and confirmed, a 50% deposit invoice is created, confirmed and paid, the delivery is validated, the balance is paid and the item is dispatched.
- The tour has no *Create Draft Invoice* step: on this order **Create Invoice** opens the draft invoice directly, so the guide goes straight to **Confirm**. The tour recorded a deposit of `600` on a `1,200.00` invoice; the automated run computes 50% of the invoice total.
- The Serial Number field only offers serials already sold to a customer (a lot filter of `is_issued = true`).
