Add or Correct an Attendance Record Manually

User manual · English · 11 steps
Learn how HR creates an attendance record by hand when an employee forgot to check in or out, choosing the employee and the exact check-in and check-out times, then saving the correction.

1. Open Attendances

Open the Attendances app.

2. Switch to List

Click List to see the records as a table.

3. New record

Click New to add an attendance record.

4. Open Employee

Click here to open the list of employees.

5. Choose the employee

Click the employee this record belongs to.

6. Check-in time

Click the check-in field to open the calendar.

7. Pick the check-in date and time

Pick the day and the time the employee started work, then click Apply.

8. Check-out time

Click the check-out field to open the calendar.

9. Pick the check-out date and time

Pick the day and the time the employee finished work, then click Apply.
